# IA-Referencias Printify Optimization

## Store Voice

IA-Referencias sells AI/dev/automation irony as merch. The strongest copy makes the contradiction visible:

- AI governance sold as ritual.
- Automation needing a human prop.
- Capitalism selling its own critique.
- Developer culture turning pain into a product.
- Anti-AI jokes that still use AI aesthetics.

Tone: sharp, sarcastic, self-aware, specific. Avoid generic "funny tech shirt" copy unless a marketplace field needs plain search language.

## Title Patterns

Good title structures:

- `<Exact Phrase> AI <Visual Hook> Tee`
- `<Exact Phrase> Developer Humor Tee`
- `<Exact Phrase> Tech Satire Shirt`
- `<Exact Phrase> <Object/Icon> Tee - Light`

Examples:

- `Human In The Loop AI Groundhog Tee`
- `Rest In Prompt AI Afterlife Tee`
- `My Voice Has 2FA Developer Humor Tee`
- `False Positive Human AI Detector Tee`

Avoid:

- Overlong titles with every keyword.
- Claims like `compliance tee`, `safe AI tee`, `guaranteed`, `profit`, `official`.
- Ambiguous suffixes unless they map to a real variant split.

## Description Structure

Use this shape:

```html
<p><strong>{Title}</strong></p>
<p>{One sharp paragraph explaining the joke and contradiction.}</p>
<ul>
  <li>{Product family / material route}</li>
  <li>{Fulfillment/provider route}</li>
  <li>{Print method and art file quality}</li>
  <li>{Ink/color strategy}</li>
  <li>{Audience/concept}</li>
</ul>
<p>{Short no-claims or final sarcastic closer.}</p>
```

Keep descriptions defensible. Satire is allowed; factual claims need sources. Do not claim a product improves compliance, productivity, income, safety, legality, or hiring outcomes.

## Bullets

Strong bullets cover:

- Blank/product family: `Bella+Canvas 3001 unisex jersey short sleeve tee`.
- Fulfillment route: `Printify Choice`.
- Print method and file: `One-color front DTG print from transparent 4500x5100 PNG`.
- Ink split: `White ink on dark garments` / `Black ink on light garments`.
- Joke/audience: `AI workflow satire`, `developer humor`, `tech governance joke`.

## Tags

Use a balanced tag set:

- Exact phrase: `human in the loop`
- Domain: `AI`, `AI governance`, `machine learning`, `workflow automation`
- Audience: `developer humor`, `software engineer`, `programmer gift`
- Visual/concept: `groundhog day`, `approval loop`, `oversight theater`
- Store shorthand: `SaaS`, `Sarcasm as a Service`
- Product/format: `one color design`, `tech satire`

For typo/surreal variants, explicitly tag the typo phrase so it looks intentional:

- `lumman in the hoop`
- `typo humor`
- `absurdist tech`
- `surreal merch`

## Pricing Rules

For Etsy-bound POD products, model:

- Printify cost by size.
- Listing/renewal allowance, commonly USD 0.20.
- Etsy transaction fee on order total.
- Etsy Payments processing fee for seller location.
- Offsite Ads as a downside scenario when relevant.

Use official current Etsy pages, not memory, before changing prices.

Default working target for shirts:

- Maintain at least 55% standard item-only profit margin after Printify cost and standard Etsy fees on every enabled size/color group.
- Use `(item_price - printify_cost - listing_fee - transaction_fee - payments_fee) / item_price` as the standard margin formula.
- Use the highest enabled Printify cost for each advertised size/variant group; do not let lower-cost colors hide an expensive variant.
- Keep S-XL as low as possible after the 55% margin floor is met.
- Increase 2XL/3XL rather than flattening large-size costs into every buyer's price.
- Disable or split variants that cannot meet the 55% floor at a credible marketplace price.
- If Offsite Ads can apply, keep downside profit positive enough to avoid regret; do not optimize solely for offsite ads unless the shop is ad-dependent.

Always state exclusions: shipping, free-shipping subsidy, tax, VAT, currency conversion, Etsy Ads, samples, discounts, returns, and failed fulfillments.

## Variant Strategy

Use dark/light split products when a one-color design needs separate white and black ink files. Name the light route clearly if both drafts may be visible.

Prefer fewer strong colors over every possible color:

- Dark routes: Black, Vintage Black, Asphalt, Navy, Deep Teal, Maroon when mockups read well.
- Light routes: White, Natural, Ash, Athletic Heather when black ink reads well.

Disable colors that make the joke hard to read or look like an accidental low-contrast print.
