# Design brief and comparison record

Copy this template into the design study's `RESOURCES.md` or adapt its existing working document. Fill in the fields relevant to the requested stage. The skill's examples illustrate design hypotheses. Product acceptance follows the store's review process.

## Brief

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Message | Exact phrase, language and what the viewer should see first. |
| Contradiction | The visual relationship that makes the satire work. |
| Destination | Store, product and surface, color and requested stage. |
| Family | Style ID and a short reason for choosing it. |
| Constraints | Motifs to preserve, exclusions and current decisions. |
| References | File or URL, timestamp where relevant, useful technique and adaptation. |
| Assets | Creator or source, usage permission, asset ID and changes. |
| Artwork | Editable master, fonts and source files, outputs and versions. |

## Comparable hypotheses

| Variant | Decision being tested | Hold constant | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Control | Current design or clean baseline | Wording and surface | Exact path |
| A | Change in hierarchy or structure | Wording and surface | Exact path |
| B | Another solution to the same weakness | Wording and surface | Exact path |
| C, when relevant | Another useful hypothesis | Wording and surface | Exact path |

An existing control can count as one of three approaches when it tests a distinct hypothesis. A before-and-after comparison can be sufficient for a targeted correction.

## Review evidence

- Open the artwork and its surface view. For an existing product, open the real mockup for the exact version and check its provenance.
- Compare artwork at 300 and 600 px, then compare whole-product views at the same widths. Preserve framing and record any crops.
- Inspect each critical area separately at 100%. A large sheet fitted to a window can appear at a different scale.
- Use the evidence contract and script when a reproducible review sheet would help. Treat measurements as prompts for visual inspection. Taste, human legibility and likelihood of purchase require separate judgment or evidence. [Evidence contract](../../product-design-critic/references/review-contract.md)

## Decision

Record the file and area, observation, effect on the message, change made and verification. Prioritize a lost punchline, incorrect wording, a bad crop or insufficient contrast before surface finish.

Record the applicable stage alongside the `product-design-critic` decision.

- `advance_to_next_stage` identifies the next step supported by evidence.
- `revise_execution` retains the idea and corrects a visible execution problem.
- `rework_concept` changes the visual relationship that fails to communicate the brief.
- `insufficient_evidence` names what is missing and what could be reviewed.

Finish with the reason for the choice, limitations, parameters needed to reproduce the output and one specific lesson for the next design. State when the review was your own. The study's status and product acceptance still follow the store's current rules.
