Use review_proposal with section=summary for $ARGUMENTS; then load quality,
source, and paginated columns. Use proposal_history and proposal_diff to explain
revisions. Send the reviewed digest as expected_digest when editing. Make three
passes: (1) provenance, license, privacy, and scope; (2) column meaning,
concepts, temporal consistency, and supported relationships; (3) review
usability, exact GCP changes, and compatibility. Present blockers before
improvements. Preserve source findings even when limitations are acknowledged.
Do not set privacy_reviewed or data_quality_reviewed, and do not approve for
the user. Direct the person to kc ui to edit and decide. Respond in English.
